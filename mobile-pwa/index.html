                        Call by name

                        Say the name, or the name and your request in one go: “Atlas, brief me”.

                        After answering something you said, Atlas listens for your reply without its name. Stay quiet or say “thanks” to finish.

                        On iPhone this works while Atlas is open on the screen. When the phone is locked, say “Hey Siri, Atlas” instead (a Siri shortcut, see docs/VOICE_SHORTCUTS.md).

                        This browser can't listen in the background. Use Chrome, or tap the stars.
